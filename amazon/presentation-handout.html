Bar lengths are drawn from the measured relations: INT4 is 62% below FP16, and INT8 is 53% above INT4.

Three takeaways

  1. Fewer bits per number means less memory.
  2. Measure quality on your own task, not just a general exam.
  3. The middle option is not always the safe one. Benchmark.
