# Maintaining the Amazon preparation workspace

The pages are implemented. Read `amazon/README.md` for current routes and checks.

When extending this module:

- Use the reviewed `amazon/data/profile.json` and the candidate-supplied resume for source alignment. Do not import assumptions from archived company prep.
- Keep role level, team, assessment status, dates, and interview format unconfirmed until supplied by the candidate.
- Label proposed AWS architectures separately from the candidate's actual deployments.
- Never invent a failure, disagreement, mentoring episode, rollout detail, customer quote, or measurement method. Use confirmation prompts and browser-local drafts.
- Verify changing AWS behavior against official documentation and include source links.
- Keep the two-week plan focused on customer discovery, justified service choices, reliability, security, cost, and credible STAR delivery.
- Update tests before changing behavior. Run the checks in `README.md`, regenerate `stories.md`, and verify critical flows in the browser.
- Keep private records and contact information out of static assets.

The later user-supplied recruiter screenshots establish AWSI - ETP, a passed OA, conditional USC interviews, and a tentative Wednesday hold. Preserve those facts separately from the unresolved exact date, time zone, room, and interview structure. Do not copy recruiter contact details or candidate intake answers into static assets.
